# RQ4 — FFN activation, gating, and depth extension

## Protocol

The study runs on native Yambda-500M with all 37,018 evaluation users. It fixes
the item and model width at 64, the μP base/delta widths at 16/32, embedding LR
at 0.064, batch size at 1280, FFN dropout at 0.1, and every non-FFN model and
data setting. Plain ReLU/GELU/SiLU FFNs use width 192 and two matrix paths;
gated ReGLU/GEGLU/SwiGLU FFNs use width 114 and three matrix paths, giving the
same 342 matrix-width units per block. Gated arms explicitly enable internal
FFN dropout, including the dropout-matched SwiGLU treatment.

Each family/depth independently tunes deep LR over 0.006, 0.012 and 0.024.
ReLU-2, GELU-2, SiLU-2, ReGLU-2, GELU-4 and GELU-8 additionally test 0.048 and
0.096 after the initial best reached the upper boundary. All ten selected rates
are interior to their completed surfaces. Every run completes the declared
linear 20-epoch horizon under cap 40, validates each epoch, restores the best
validation checkpoint, and records `selection_resolved: true` for its training
horizon.

The complete generated 42-row surface is in the [tuning
ledger](../scratchpad/rq4_activation_depth_tuning_500m.md). The reader tables
are generated by [rq4_activation_depth.py](../analysis/rq4_activation_depth.py),
which constructs every accepted directory name exactly and selects rates by
the maximum `epoch/val_true.recall@100` parsed from each `sweep.log`. Final
metrics are reported only after validation selects the run. The generator
never scans the broad `g1_rqtune_rqfinal_ffn_gelu171_*_500m` glob.

The only reused artifacts are pinned exactly:

- `g1_rqtune_rqfinal_ffn_gelu171_e0p064_d0p006_b1280_cap40_ts2_r3_500m`
- `g1_rqtune_rqfinal_ffn_gelu171_e0p064_d0p012_b1280_cap40_ts2_r3_500m`
- `g1_rqtune_rqfinal_ffn_gelu171_e0p064_d0p024_b1280_cap40_ts2_r3_500m`

Three historical GELU-192 directories with other embedding rates or training
semantics remain preserved in raw storage and are intentionally ineligible for
this comparison.

## Comparisons

Each gated family is directionally ahead of its
activation-matched plain counterpart. ReGLU minus ReLU is +0.00236933 final
recall and +0.00177614 final NDCG. GEGLU minus GELU is +0.00236157 recall and
+0.00159821 NDCG. SwiGLU minus SiLU is +0.00460228
recall and +0.00174725 NDCG. Under the reader thresholds, all three NDCG gains
and the SwiGLU-versus-SiLU recall gain resolve; the other two recall gains
remain unresolved.

Dropout-matched SwiGLU minus GELU is +0.00224723 recall (+1.67%) at two layers,
+0.00099655 (+0.74%) at four layers, and +0.00192043 (+1.40%) at eight layers.
All three are below the 0.003 reader threshold. The corresponding NDCG changes
are +0.00104134, +0.00010011 and +0.00143299, so the two-layer and eight-layer
NDCG gains resolve. The relative recall advantage does not grow monotonically
with depth.

GEGLU exceeds SwiGLU by 0.00011433 final recall at two layers, well inside the
reader threshold. The local study therefore supports gated FFNs as a family
but does not separate GEGLU from SwiGLU or prove that SwiGLU uniquely wins. The
result is consistent with the gated-FFN findings in [Shazeer
(2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05202) and with the common use of SwiGLU in
modern LLMs.
