========================================================================
HIGH-LEVEL REVIEW (dcn/) — 2026-06-28
Big-block / missing-functionality / convenience items. Mid-level interface
nits are inline FIXMEs in the code, not here.
========================================================================

[training] Embedding-freeze during pretrain is half-built. CombinedOptimizer
  has set_enabled() (only exercised in its own unit test), the trainer has a
  FIXME "add freeze to part of the model", but nothing wires it and
  PretrainConfig has no freeze knob. Either finish (config field -> trainer
  toggles set_enabled at the right epoch) or remove the scaffolding.

[architecture] Dead config-driven layer-building subsystem contradicts the
  config-as-code design: layer_registry / build_layer / ConfigurableLayer.
  build_layer is never called, only SwiGLU is registered, and models are built
  directly in Python. The string-config factory classes are gone (per review):
  ActivationFactory / NormalizationFactory are now plain callable aliases that
  match what crossnet/resnet are really passed. What remains to decide is the
  registry path itself — delete it, or commit to it.

[data] EventDataset loads each day fully into RAM
  (pl.concat([read_parquet ...]).to_numpy()). Fine for day-by-day on 50m, but
  it's the scaling ceiling for 500m/5b — note it before pointing the framework
  at the larger sizes. SequenceDataset already spills into RAM-bounded buckets;
  the event path could borrow that.
